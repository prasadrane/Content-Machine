# Whole-product review: editorial trust and integrity

Date: 2026-11-05. Planning only; independent product-owner and editorial-reliability critique.
Basis: brief (all six confirmed answers), proposed workflow plan and ALL specialist reports 01-05; specialist source inspections supply current-behavior evidence with their stated graph freshness limits.
I used codebase-memory for orientation and consulted architecture/spike context; no additional structural claims are made.
No browser audit, product test, live generation, deployment, or representative-creator research was performed.

## Verdict

Proceed with the focused workflow direction; the current source-backed gaps prevent an honest private hosted launch.
The promise is “finish content you knowingly approve.” Real records and clear handoffs establish trust; more badges, checkboxes or compliance screens would weaken this journey.

## T2 — Resolve contradictions once, in the shared item contract

I11/D10/E9/N3 still leave startup open; E4 and N14 leave Instagram/audience questions open. The brief resolves them.
Use saved ideas/drafts plus Resume; equal own/discovery entry; explicit AI skip; technical creators; Instagram captions AND Reel scripts.
One item owns intake, sources, platform/format, text, snapshots, reviews, approvals and export events; discovered sources become authored work only through deliberate selection (I8/N4).
Keep stable internal identities; a title/slug, peak score, filename or matching text cannot establish lineage (D5/E9/N6).

## T3 — Change: make provenance useful without turning intake into an evidence form

I6/D2/S6 expose the same risk: third-party evidence or a prefilled persona can become a first-person claim.
Lead with editable notes or an untouched pasted draft; show source title/link/excerpt beside it and one optional “Your point” field.
Store source, creator experience and interpretation distinctly; permit attributed technical commentary with no personal anecdote or metric.
Writing/revision receives that context; title-only sources say so and missing support remains an editable gap. Settings changes affect future requests without rewriting saved artifacts/context (S4/S5).

## T4 — Change: review status follows the actual reviewed text

D7 identifies sanitizing after scoring; D5 identifies incomparable raw/normalized scores. Neither can support a current /10 trust signal.
Bind each review to the submitted immutable text snapshot and format; show any subsequent sanitizer/wording change in comparison.
If returned text differs, identify the review as belonging to the earlier version; accepting changes creates a new working version.
Do not automatically rerun AI to repair this status: the creator can review again or explicitly continue without current AI advice.
Keep useful suggested edits first; score basis and judge details are secondary. Scores never establish factual accuracy or authorship.

## T5 — Simplify: one human decision approves one complete deliverable

D10/E3/N2 correctly separate optional AI advice from required human approval; failure must never be relabeled as successful review or skip.
Offer Review with AI and Continue without AI review; after failure offer Retry or deliberate Continue without review, preserving the failure record.
Approval shows the complete selected-platform artifact, actual review status and expandable evidence/changes, then one Approve this version action.
Reject a separate ritual acknowledgment checkbox or repeated export confirmation; the clearly labeled affirmative action records consent.
Bind approval to creator, item, immutable version, exact text/structured parts, destination and format; later edits or format changes need new approval.
An edit followed by identical wording cannot resurrect an old approval; the historical approved snapshot remains exportable (E2/E9).

## T6 — Keep and tighten: export is retrieval; adaptation is new editorial work

E1/E2/E8 conflict with today's anchor-then-bundle behavior: preparing the platform artifact must finish before approval.
Stage 4 retrieves the approved snapshot without a model call, cleanup, shortening or regeneration; thread boundaries are part of that snapshot.
Record Copy succeeded or Download started only from the real operation; neither means downloaded-to-disk verification or publication (E6/N14).
On clipboard/download failure retain selectable approved text and offer the other method; do not lock the creator behind a failed API.
Adapt for another platform creates a linked unapproved branch with its own format, versions and approval; cancellation/failure leaves the original usable.
Instagram caption and Reel script are explicit separate format choices; do not silently combine them into a package or substitute a generic script (E4).

## T7 — Add: the smallest private save contract, before hosted real drafts

N4/N5/E9/S8 make authentication, server-enforced ownership and durable storage release prerequisites, not decorative Settings features.
Enforce ownership for item, source attachment, settings, history, async result and export/file access; private URLs or filtered lists are insufficient.
Persist working edits independently of AI; acknowledge Saving / Saved at time / Not saved with Retry from actual writes, retaining unsaved text.
Snapshot at review, accepted revision, approval, restore and adaptation; read-only history offers Restore as new version without inheriting approval or losing work (N6). No visible audit event per keystroke is needed.
No vendor, team roles, distributed worker tier or offline-sync system is required by this product decision.

## T8 — Change: truthful records and safe migration replace demonstration behavior

Remove seeded authors, anecdotes, scores, reviews and unrelated replay from real work; keep offline test fixtures isolated (I10/D8/E7/S6/S8).
Distinguish loading, no records, no matches, partial success and failure; retries preserve inputs and previous usable results (I9/N11/S9).
Known demo provenance may move records out of normal work into a labeled legacy area; ambiguous historical records remain preserved.
Before migration, preserve a recoverable copy; rerunning migration must not duplicate items, overwrite edited preferences or invent ownership/approval.
Import only into an explicitly established owner's workspace; unmatched exports show “Source/approval association unavailable,” never a guessed peak draft.
Comments stays retired while its historical records remain intact; seed updates cannot replace creator-owned settings or content (E9/S8).

## T9 — Keep: Lessons and Settings support completion without supervising it

Ship optional editable author facts, enabled sources and manual writing preferences; blank means blank, never another creator's identity (S3-S7).
Save and use is approval for a manually written preference; inferred proposals remain pending, with plain conflict outcomes and preserved input.
Edit/Stop using preserves history; factual corrections stay item-specific unless deliberately generalized (S2/S4). Failed load/save preserves fields and reports failure; return restores the item (S9/N7).
Keep Lessons reachable after export; defer inference, raw prompt displays and semantic dashboards rather than making them publishing prerequisites.

## T10 — Smallest honest launch and acceptance boundary

Launch the complete private saved-work journey, one editor, optional AI review, exact approval, immutable export and minimal Settings/manual Lessons.
Propose LinkedIn post plus Instagram caption/Reel initially; defer X/newsletter/YouTube until the same contract is tested. Preserve their records/exports; this scope reduction does not claim existing formats are unsupported.
New content → one platform/format → equally prominent notes/draft or discovery → develop → review or skip → approve → copy/download → saved list.
No profile onboarding, personal metric, Council pass, adaptation, lesson creation or publication claim is required to finish.

## T11 — End-to-end acceptance cases (proposed, not executed)

1. Empty private A: paste a technical draft without profile/sources, skip AI, approve and copy exact text; no persona, score or sample appears (I1/D10/E3/S6).
2. A discovers an article with no personal example; source/excerpt and commentary survive generation, approval and restart without a first-person claim (I6/D2/E11/N5).
3. Dictate into A, switch to B during transcription/review, then resolve both: B stays unchanged and A receives only version/field-owned results (I7/D8/N10).
4. Review version 2, sanitize/revise to 3, then approve 3: preview labels the old review honestly; export equals 3 and version 2 remains inspectable (D7/E1/E9).
5. Save fails or library/AI/source fetch fails: preserve text/partial results, show the actual failure, retry safely, or continue manually to approval (I9/I10/N11/S9).
6. Save profile/source/preference plus draft and approval; restart/deploy and recover acknowledged records; B cannot read/write A through direct routes/files (N4/N5/S7/S8).
7. Approve Instagram caption, adapt to Reel: separate editable script and approval; cancelling adaptation preserves caption export; neither implies rendered video (E4/E8).
8. Change a preference, reject its conflict, restore an old draft and migrate legacy data twice: no silent activation, overwrite, approval revival or Comments loss (S3/S4/E9/N6).
9. Keyboard/screen-reader and 320px/200% resize: find Resume, inspect full preview, skip AI, approve and recover from clipboard failure with readable status/focus (I12/E10/N12/N13).

## T12 — Release decision and remaining questions

Must have: T2-T8 identity, grounding, current-text status, single human approval, unchanged export, private saves and preservation; T9 truthful minimal support.
Defer advanced preferences/inference, additional platform formats, analytics, automatic publishing and team/public features; do not add generic compliance gates.
All six creator questions are settled; T10's format narrowing remains a reviewer proposal. Implementation, migration rehearsal, ownership and rendered/accessibility checks precede any private hosted release claim.
